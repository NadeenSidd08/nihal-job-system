          3  ·  The Honest Gaps ▼
          Primary Gap — Multi-Site Operations Oversight

          The JD requires 3+ years directly overseeing nursing operations across multiple hospital sites concurrently. Priya has never done this. Lakeside is technically a "two-hospital system," but Priya manages one ED at one site; she has no direct oversight of the second hospital's nursing operations. This is a real gap, not a technicality, and Renata's own background (informatics, multi-system rollouts) makes it likely to come up directly.

          How to handle it out loud: Name it in the first sentence of any answer that touches it. Don't reframe "single-site" as something it isn't. Then bridge immediately to the two closest real analogs: validating a clinical protocol standard across a hospital-wide EHR migration at Dellview (Cerner to Epic — many units, one shared standard, zero rollback incidents on the pieces Priya owned), and closing an accreditation finding start to finish at Lakeside. Both are honest evidence of the underlying skill (building a standard that holds across more stakeholders than any one manager controls) without claiming the specific multi-site experience she doesn't have.

          Secondary Gap — Board-Level Presentation

          The JD asks for comfort presenting to executive leadership and board-level committees. Priya's closest experience is the standing monthly relationship with Lakeside's quality and patient-safety office, which is executive-adjacent but not the board itself. This is a softer, more coachable gap than the multi-site one; if asked directly, say so plainly rather than stretching the quality-office relationship into something it wasn't.
