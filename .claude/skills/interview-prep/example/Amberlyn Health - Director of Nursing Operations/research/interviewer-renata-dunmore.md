## What she'll likely probe

- **The multi-site gap, directly.** Priya's background is single-site (one ED, one hospital's
  trauma designation). Renata's own bio emphasizes cross-system rollout experience, and the JD's
  multi-site requirement reads like it was written with her lens in mind. Expect a direct question
  like "tell me about a time you standardized something across more than one site" — Priya doesn't
  have that story and shouldn't invent one. See the deck's Honest Gaps panel for how to answer this
  without either dodging or overclaiming.
- **Standardization philosophy.** Given her informatics background, expect questions about *how* to
  roll out a shared standard across sites with different existing practices, not just *whether* to.
- **Budget rigor at scale.** Her current title (Patient Care Services, not Finance) suggests she'll
  care more about operational judgment than spreadsheet mechanics, but $5M+ budget ownership will
  still come up given the JD's explicit floor.
- **EHR/systems fluency.** Her informatics background makes the Dellview Cerner-to-Epic story
  unusually relevant to her personally, more than to a typical hiring manager.

## What to ask her back

- How is decision authority actually split between this role and each site's CNO once a standard is
  set: who resolves it when a site CNO disagrees with the system standard?
- What does "success in year one" look like to her specifically, beyond what's written in the
  posting?
- How is the Westgate integration going from the nursing staff's side right now, not just the
  operational side: what's the honest temperature in the room?

  Services." The *Regional Health Quarterly* profile, published roughly 8 months ago, refers to her
  as "VP, Nursing Operations." Both are plausible: this could reflect a title change after a reorg
  that the profile predates, or an inconsistency in how the trade press described her role at the
  time. This dossier defaults to "VP, Patient Care Services" because it's Amberlyn's own current
  page, but that is a guess, not a confirmed fact. Do not correct her if she introduces herself with
  either title.
- **Tenure length, imprecise.** The Amberlyn bio says she has been "with the system for the past
  four years." The Great Lakes Nursing Executives Forum bio, from roughly a year before this
  interview, describes her as having "nearly five years at Amberlyn" at that time, which would put
  her closer to six years now. The two don't reconcile cleanly; treat "around four to six years" as
  the honest range rather than picking one.
