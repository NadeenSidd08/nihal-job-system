**Last updated:** 2026-08 (initial build; no prior rounds yet)

## Market position

Amberlyn Health System is the mid-sized, not-for-profit player in a three-way Corrigan Falls metro
market:

- **Corrigan Falls University Hospital** — the academic medical center, strongest in complex/tertiary
  referrals and research funding, weaker on community-level access. Amberlyn's flagship (Amberlyn
  Medical Center) competes directly with it for trauma and cardiac cases.
- **Meridian Crest Health** — a larger, for-profit regional chain with five facilities in the metro
  and surrounding counties, aggressive on urgent-care and outpatient footprint expansion.
- **Amberlyn Health System** (subject of this brief) — positions itself as the "system that still
  feels local," leaning on long-standing community ties at Amberlyn North and now Westgate, while
  using the flagship's Level I trauma designation as its clinical anchor.

[Source: fictional "Corrigan Falls Metro Healthcare Landscape" market analysis, trade publication
*Regional Health Quarterly*, fictional, dated 2026-03.]

## Ownership and funding

Amberlyn is a not-for-profit system governed by a community board. The Westgate acquisition
(completed roughly 18 months before this posting) was financed primarily through a municipal bond
issuance rather than an equity transaction — consistent with Amberlyn's not-for-profit structure.

## Strategic read

This role sits at the exact seam the Westgate acquisition opened up: three campuses, three nursing
cultures, one accreditation finding demanding a shared standard. Amberlyn is not looking for a
policy writer — it already has policies. It's looking for someone who has personally rebuilt a
nursing operations process after an external body (a state survey, in Priya's case) found it
wanting, and can do that work at three-site scale without alienating any one site's staff.
