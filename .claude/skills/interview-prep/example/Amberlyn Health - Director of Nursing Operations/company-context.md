## What Amberlyn does

Amberlyn Health System is a not-for-profit, three-hospital regional system anchored in Corrigan
Falls. It runs the metro area's only Level I trauma center (at the flagship Amberlyn Medical
Center), plus two smaller community hospitals — Amberlyn North and the recently acquired Amberlyn
Westgate. It makes money the way most not-for-profit systems do: a mix of commercial payer
contracts, Medicare/Medicaid reimbursement, and philanthropic/bond financing for capital projects
like the Westgate acquisition itself. [Source: Amberlyn Health System "About Us" and investor/bond
disclosure page, fictional, accessed 2026-08.]

## Why this role exists now

Two things collided in the last 18 months: the Westgate acquisition, which brought a third campus
with its own staffing and onboarding culture into the system overnight, and a system-wide
accreditation survey that flagged inconsistent competency-documentation practices across all three
sites as a corrective-action item. Nobody currently owns standardizing nursing operations across
all three campuses — each site's CNO runs their own shop. This role is Amberlyn's answer to that
gap. [Source: `research/company-brief.md`, "Recent signal" section.]

## Talking points that land

- **The Westgate integration is the actual subtext of this interview.** Every question about
  "multi-site" is really a question about whether you can hold three cultures to one standard
  without steamrolling any of them. Lead with respect for site-level context, not a one-size-fits-
  all pitch.
- **The accreditation finding is a gift, not a landmine.** It's the closest one-to-one match in the
  whole JD to Priya's Lakeside state-survey resolution. Name it directly and early.
- **Budget fluency matters more here than at a single-site manager job.** $5M+ managed budget is a
  floor for this role, not a highlight — be ready to talk about how a budget conversation changes
  when it spans three sites with three different cost structures.

## Honest watch-outs

- Priya has never directly overseen nursing operations across more than one site concurrently — the
  JD's multi-site requirement is a real gap, not a rounding error. See the deck's Honest Gaps panel
  for how to handle this out loud.
- Post-acquisition integrations are politically sensitive. Assume some Westgate nursing leadership
  feels standardization efforts imposed from the flagship campus are unwelcome. Don't promise a
  fast, clean rollout; the honest answer is that it will be uneven and needs a change-management
  plan, not just a policy document.
- Renata's own title appears inconsistently across sources (see the interviewer dossier's
  uncertainty register). Use "VP, Patient Care Services" — the title on Amberlyn's own site — unless
  she introduces herself differently.
