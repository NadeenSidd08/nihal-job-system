# Director of Nursing Operations

**Organization:** Amberlyn Health System
**Location:** Corrigan Falls (on-site, with regular travel to Amberlyn North and Amberlyn Westgate campuses)
**Reports to:** VP, Patient Care Services
**Comp band:** $148,000–$178,000 base, plus annual incentive target up to 15%. Relocation assistance available for qualified candidates.
**Employment type:** Full-time, exempt

> All company and role details on this page are fictional, built for a worked example of the
> interview-prep skill. Amberlyn Health System does not exist.

## About Amberlyn Health System

Amberlyn Health System is a three-hospital, not-for-profit regional health system serving the
Corrigan Falls metro area (population approximately 900,000). The system includes Amberlyn Medical
Center (420 beds, flagship and tertiary referral site), Amberlyn North Community Hospital (180 beds),
and Amberlyn Westgate Hospital (140 beds, acquired 18 months ago). Amberlyn employs roughly 14,000
people system-wide, including approximately 3,100 nursing staff.

## Why this role exists now

The Westgate acquisition brought a third campus, a third nursing culture, and a third set of
staffing, onboarding, and quality-reporting practices into the system inside 18 months. A recent
system-wide accreditation survey flagged inconsistent competency-documentation practices across the
three campuses as a finding requiring a corrective action plan. This is a newly created role: no one
currently owns nursing operations standardization across all three sites end to end.

## What you'll own

- Standardize staffing models, scheduling practices, and competency/onboarding programs across
  Amberlyn Medical Center, Amberlyn North, and Amberlyn Westgate
- Own the system-level nursing operations labor budget in partnership with each site's CNO and the
  system CFO's office
- Lead the corrective action plan for the recent accreditation finding on competency documentation,
  and build the ongoing quality/safety reporting cadence that keeps it closed
- Partner with each site's CNO and nurse managers on cross-site EHR and clinical-systems
  optimization work as Westgate finishes migrating onto the system's shared platform
- Represent nursing operations on the system Quality & Patient Safety Committee
- Build the operating rhythm (dashboards, reporting cadence, escalation paths) that lets nursing
  leadership at all three sites work from one shared picture of staffing, competency, and quality

## Requirements

- BSN required; MSN or equivalent graduate degree in nursing leadership, administration, or a
  related field strongly preferred
- Active, unrestricted RN license
- 5+ years of progressive nursing leadership experience, including experience with direct reports
  at the charge or manager level or above
- **3+ years directly overseeing nursing operations across multiple hospital sites or facilities
  concurrently** — this is a multi-site operations role, not a single-department one
- Demonstrated ownership of a nursing labor budget of $5M or more
- Experience partnering on a clinical systems or EHR transition (implementation, migration, or
  major upgrade)
- Experience leading a response to a regulatory or accreditation survey finding, through resolution
  and re-survey
- Certified Emergency Nurse (CEN), CNML, or equivalent nursing administration certification
  preferred
- Comfort presenting operational and quality data to executive leadership and board-level
  committees

## What success looks like in year one

A single, shared staffing and competency-tracking standard live across all three campuses; the
accreditation finding closed with zero repeat findings on re-survey; and a nursing operations
reporting cadence that the system CNO and site CNOs can all point to as the source of truth.
