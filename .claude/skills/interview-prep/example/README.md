# Worked Example: One Application Folder

This folder demonstrates the interview-prep skill's core convention: **one folder per job
application**, holding everything for that pursuit in one place. Every organization and person
named below — Amberlyn Health System, its leadership, its competitors, Priya Fenwick, Renata
Dunmore — is fictional, invented for this example. No real company, hospital, or individual is
described anywhere in this folder.

## The scenario

Priya Fenwick is interviewing for **Director of Nursing Operations** at **Amberlyn Health System**,
a fictional three-hospital regional system. This is **Round 1**, with a named interviewer, Renata
Dunmore (VP, Patient Care Services), which triggers the interviewer-dossier research branch
(SKILL.md steps 11-12) alongside the company brief.

## The convention

`Amberlyn Health - Director of Nursing Operations/` is the whole application: the two files Priya
supplies before the skill ever runs — `tailored-resume.md` (her resume, tailored to this posting)
and `job-post.md` (the posting itself) — sit alongside everything the skill generates from them:
`company-context.md`, `research/company-brief.md`, `research/interviewer-renata-dunmore.md`, and
`interview-prep.html`.

## Why `tailored-resume.md` matters most

In this repo, `tailored-resume.md` is the sole source of truth for every career claim on the deck —
there's no separate career-notes file to fall back on. It's deliberately thinner than a full career
history: results, not the situation or judgment call behind them. Every number on
`interview-prep.html` — the budget, the metrics, the dates, the credentials — traces back to a line
in this one file; company-side figures (Amberlyn's beds, headcount, comp band) trace instead to
`job-post.md` and `research/`.
