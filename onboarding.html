1Check your Mac and Claude plan

  1. Use a Mac.
  2. Install the Claude desktop app. claude.ai/download (opens in a new tab)
  3. Check that you are on a paid Claude plan, Pro or Max.

Heads-up. The morning scan needs a plan that offers Auto permission mode. If you do not see Auto in step 10, stop and tell Nadeen.

2Make your Job search folder

  1. In Finder, make a new folder. Call it something like Job search.
  2. Put it anywhere easy to find, such as Documents.

Everything you gather in the next two steps goes in this folder.

3Save your LinkedIn profile as a PDF

  1. Open your own profile on LinkedIn.
  2. Click Resources, the button under your name, then click Save to PDF.
  3. Move the downloaded file into your Job search folder.

4Add your résumé and have your website link ready

  1. Put your résumé in the same folder, as a PDF or a .docx file.
  2. Have the link to your website with your clips ready. You will paste it in step 8.

Tip. If your résumé is a Google Doc, open it and click File, Download, PDF. Then move the PDF into your folder.

5Connect Google Drive, Sheets, Gmail and Indeed

  1. In the Claude app, click Customize in the left sidebar, then Connectors.
  2. Find each one below, click Connect, and sign in. A check mark means it is connected.
  • Google Drive. Claude creates your tracker Sheet here and saves your application Docs.
  • Google Sheets. Claude writes new jobs into your tracker and reads your changes.
  • Gmail. Claude reads LinkedIn job alert emails, which is how it sees jobs at sites it cannot read directly, like Politico, The Atlantic and The Hill. It never sends email.
  • Indeed. Optional, and recommended. It adds a broad set of listings to the daily search.

Heads-up. Use the same Google account for Drive and Sheets.

6Let Claude reach the job sites

This lets Claude read JournalismJobs, Mediabistro and Daybook, and download your job system. You do it once.

  1. Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar, then Settings, then Capabilities. On the web it is claude.ai/settings/capabilities (opens in a new tab).
  2. Keep Allow network egress turned on.
  3. Keep Domain allowlist on Package managers only. Never choose All domains. Job posts are text from strangers, and a short list keeps Claude from reaching anything else.
  4. Under Additional allowed domains, add the five domains below, one at a time. Click Copy, paste the domain into the box, and click Add.
*.journalismjobs.com
*.mediabistro.com
*.daybook.com
github.com
codeload.github.com

Heads-up. The box rejects several domains at once, so add them one at a time. Each domain shows in the list under the box once it is added. All five should be there. New settings only work in a chat you start after you add them, so finish this step before step 8.

7Start a chat on your folder

  1. In the Claude app, click + New.
  2. Under the message box, click Project or folder.
  3. Click Add folder, not New project, and pick your Job search folder.

Do this before you send anything.

Tip. If Claude does not recognize a command like /jobs, send it again.

8Send the setup message

  1. Type your website link below. It fills into the message. Leave it empty if you have no site.
  2. Click Copy, paste the message into the chat you started in step 7, and send it.
Message to paste into Claude

Heads-up. A Mac window may pop up asking to install “command line developer tools”. Either button is fine. Claude may also ask to read the folder above yours. Say yes. If it says a website is blocked, go back to step 6, then start a new chat on your folder and paste the message again.

9What Claude asks you

Claude works through setup one question at a time. It takes about 30 minutes. The moments where you do something are marked.

  1. Claude downloads the job system into your folder.
  2. Claude checks that it can reach the job sites.
  3. Claude makes a clean text copy of your résumé.
  4. You Claude lists your clips from your website. Approve the list, fix any rows, and star your favorites.
  5. You Claude writes a fact list from your résumé, LinkedIn and clips. Skim it, and answer 2 or 3 questions about wins that are not on paper.
  6. You Claude asks about your beats, target roles, location, salary floor and dealbreakers. It takes about 5 minutes.
  7. You Claude builds your Google Sheet and gives you the link. Bookmark it.
  8. You Claude publishes your Job Inbox and gives you the link. Bookmark it, and open it once to check.
  9. You Claude asks if you want the morning scan on or off.

Tip. If you stop halfway, start a new chat on the nihal-job-system folder and say “continue setup”. Claude picks up where you left off. From now on, start your chats on that folder. It sits inside your Job search folder.

After setup, you and Nadeen have a check-in call of about 20 minutes.

10Check the scan’s settings

  1. In the Claude app, click Scheduled in the sidebar, click your task, then click Edit.
  2. Set the Working folder to your nihal-job-system folder. It is the folder row at the bottom of the Instructions box. Save, open the task again, and check that Folder now ends in nihal-job-system.
  3. Set the Model to Sonnet. It is the Default menu at the bottom right.
  4. Set the Permission mode to Auto. It is the Manual button at the bottom left. Auto lets the scan run without stopping to ask you.

Heads-up. If Auto is not in the list, stop and tell Nadeen. Your plan may not include it.

If Claude never offered to set up the scan, paste this message into a chat on your nihal-job-system folder.

Set up my morning scan.

11Keep your Mac awake and do one test run

  1. Go to Settings, then This computer, then System, and turn on Keep computer awake.
  2. Open your scan task and click Run now once.
  3. Leave it alone for about 10 minutes. It should say succeeded and show a 3-line summary.

Heads-up. Closing the lid still puts your Mac to sleep. The scan only runs while the Claude app is open and your Mac is awake. If the test run stops to ask you something, tell Nadeen what it named.

12Set up LinkedIn job alerts

Some employers block Claude from reading their job pages. LinkedIn alert emails are how it sees jobs at Politico, The Atlantic and The Hill.

  1. On linkedin.com, click Jobs and search for a role you want. Set the location to Washington, DC.
  2. Turn on the Set alert switch.
  3. Choose Daily and Email.
  4. Repeat for two or three roles.

13Open your Job Inbox

  1. Open the Job Inbox link Claude gave you in step 9. Bookmark it.
  2. The Inbox tab shows new jobs. The Pipeline tab shows jobs you are working on.
  3. On each card, Save keeps a job, Pass drops it, Build in Claude copies a command, and Open posting opens the job.

Tip. The Inbox reads your Google Sheet through your Google Sheets connector. If it cannot open your Sheet, check that the connector uses the same Google account as your Drive.

14Build an application with /apply

  1. Click Build in Claude on a job card. It copies a command. Paste it into a chat on your nihal-job-system folder. You can also type /apply and the job, or paste a link to a posting.
  2. Claude shows a short list of what it will build. Say yes.
  3. Claude builds a résumé Doc and a cover letter Doc in your Drive, in a folder called Job applications. It picks 3 to 5 of your clips and lists any claims it wants you to confirm.
  4. The job’s status becomes Ready to apply.

Heads-up. Read the claims to confirm. Fix anything that is wrong before you send. Claude only uses facts from your fact list.

15Edit, download, apply, tell Claude

  1. Open the two Docs and edit them until they sound like you. Then click File, Download, PDF.
  2. Apply on the employer’s own site. Claude never applies for you.
  3. Click I applied on the job’s card in the Ready to apply column of the Pipeline tab, or tell Claude “I applied to The Bulwark”.

16Your 10-minute morning skim

  1. If your scan is on, it runs weekdays at about 7:30 and adds up to 10 new jobs, each with status New.
  2. Open your Job Inbox. Save or Pass each job.
  3. Build the best one with Build in Claude.
  • Say show me more to add the next 10 matches. The others wait in your Sheet for 14 days. Say show me more to add them.
  • Say find me jobs to run a scan any time.
  • Once a week, say my top 3 this week. Claude builds applications for your three best jobs. It shows you the three first, and you confirm or swap them before it builds.

17What each status means

Your Google Sheet keeps every job and its status. Ready to apply means the ball is with you. Applied means you sent it.

StatusMeaningWho sets it
NewFound by a scan. You have not looked at it yet.Claude’s search
SavedYou want to keep it.You
Ready to applyClaude built your application and it is waiting for you to send it.Claude
AppliedYou sent it.Only you. Claude never marks it Applied on its own.
InterviewingYou are in a hiring process.You
OfferYou have an offer.You
PassedYou are not interested.You
ClosedThe posting is gone or the process ended.You, or Claude when you say so

19If something goes wrong

  • Claude cannot find a command like /jobs. Send it again. Check that your chat is on the nihal-job-system folder.
  • Claude says a website is blocked. Go back to step 6 and check all five domains. Then start a new chat on your folder and paste the step 8 message again.
  • You cannot find Connectors. Click Customize in the left sidebar, then Connectors.
  • The scan stalled. A prompt is waiting for an answer. Check that the task’s Permission mode is still Auto (Scheduled, your task, Edit). Then open the run from the task’s History list and answer the prompt.
  • The app shows a yellow “sign in again” banner. Sign in again, then click Run now once.
  • The scan did not run. The app was closed or the Mac was asleep. Open the app and wake the Mac. One catch-up run happens on its own. Check that Keep computer awake is on.
  • The summary says “no LinkedIn alert emails”. No alert emails arrived from LinkedIn in the last few days. Set up the alerts in step 12, with email delivery.
  • The run was rejected. You are at your Claude plan’s usage limit. Wait for it to reset.
  • A Daybook job shows “Employer hidden (Daybook)”. Daybook shows the employer only to paid members. The link still shows a short description. To build an application, paste the description into Claude and tell it the employer if you know it. Claude will not guess.

Heads-up. Still stuck? Tell Nadeen what happened and what Claude said, word for word.
