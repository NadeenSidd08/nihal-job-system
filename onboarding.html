Job desk / Setup guide

Your Job Desk Setup

Every weekday morning, this system finds journalism jobs for you and adds them to a Google Sheet. When one looks good, it drafts a tailored résumé, a cover letter in your voice, and clip picks for that application. Setup takes about an hour in total, in a few sittings. Work through the five levels below before your check-in call with Nadeen.

Level 1 / Gather

Gather your stuff

Goal: have everything in one folder before you meet Claude.

  1. Install the Claude desktop app on your Mac. You need a paid Claude plan.
  2. Make a new folder. Call it something like "Job search".
  3. Put two files in it: your résumé (PDF or .docx), and your LinkedIn profile as a PDF. To get the PDF, open your own profile on LinkedIn, click More, then Save to PDF. Move the downloaded file into your folder.
  4. Have the link to your personal website of clips ready.
What you'll seeA folder with two files in it: your résumé and a LinkedIn PDF (usually named Profile.pdf).

Done when: the Claude app is installed, and the folder holds your résumé and your LinkedIn PDF.

Level 2 / Connect

Connect your accounts

Goal: let Claude reach your Google account and Indeed.

  1. Open the Claude app, then Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Find each connector below, click Connect, and sign in.
  • Google Drive. Claude creates your tracker Sheet here and saves your application Docs.
  • Google Sheets. Claude writes new jobs into your tracker and reads your changes.
  • Gmail. Claude reads LinkedIn job alert emails, which is how it sees jobs at sites it cannot read directly, like Politico, The Atlantic and The Hill. It never sends email.
  • Indeed. Optional but recommended. It adds a broad set of listings to the daily search.
What you'll seeEach connector shows as connected in the list. If one is missing, Claude will tell you in Level 3 and show you how to fix it.

Done when: Drive and Sheets are connected. Gmail and Indeed if you can.

Level 3 / Meet Claude / about 30 min

Meet Claude

Goal: Claude learns your work and builds your system with you.

  1. In the Claude app, open Claude in your "Job search" folder. In the Claude desktop app, open the Code tab, start a new session, and choose your Job search folder as its folder.
  2. Type your website link below. It fills into the message.
  3. Press Copy, paste the message into Claude, and send it.
  4. Answer Claude's questions. It asks one at a time.

Check your morning scan

    
Goal: new jobs land in your Sheet every weekday morning on their own.

What you'll seeThe test run finishes with "succeeded" and a 3-line summary: how many jobs were added, the best one, and anything skipped. New rows appear in your Sheet with Status New. If it stops to ask you something, tell Nadeen what it named.

    
Optional: LinkedIn job alerts
      Some employers block Claude from reading their job pages. LinkedIn alert emails are how it sees those jobs. On linkedin.com, click Jobs, search for a role you want, and set the location to Washington, DC. Turn on the Set alert switch, then choose Daily and Email. Repeat for two or three roles.
    

    
Done when: the Run now test says "succeeded". The scan only runs while the Claude app is open and your Mac is awake.

    

    

  


  
  

    
Level 5 / First application

    
Your first application

    
Goal: one real application, built by Claude and sent by you.

    
    
      
  1. Open your Google Sheet and pick a job that interests you.
    2. 
      
  2. In Claude (in the nihal-job-system folder), type /apply and the job, for example the title and organization. You can also paste a link to a job posting.
    3. 
      
  3. Claude may show a short list of what it will build. Say yes.
    4. 
      
  4. Claude builds a Google Doc résumé and a Google Doc cover letter in your Drive, in a folder called "Job applications". It also picks 3 to 5 of your clips, and lists any claims it wants you to confirm. In your Sheet the job's Status becomes Ready to apply.
    5. 
      
  5. Open the Docs and edit them until they sound like you. Then File, Download, PDF.
    6. 
      
  6. Apply on the employer's own site. Claude never applies for you.
    7.

    8.

Cheat sheet

  
Open Claude in the nihal-job-system folder. Then say any of these.

  

    

      
You sayWhat happens

      
        
/jobsA simple menu that points you to the next step. Type it whenever you forget the commands.

        
find me jobsClaude searches your sources now and adds the best new matches to your Sheet. Also /find-jobs.

        
show me moreAdds the next 10 matches that were waiting. No new search.

        
/apply plus a job or linkBuilds your résumé, cover letter and clip picks for that job.

        
I applied to XSets that job to Applied.

        
pass on XSets that job to Passed. Claude asks for a one-line reason.

        
I published something newClaude asks for the link and, once you confirm it is your piece, offers to add it to your clips.

        
update my job systemGets the newest version of the system. Your own files are not touched.

      
    

  


  
The 10-a-day cap

  
Each search adds only the 10 best new jobs to your Sheet, so it never floods. The other matches wait in Claude's memory, and they expire after 14 days. Say "show me more" to add the next 10.


  
What each Status means

  

    

      
StatusMeaning

      
        
NewFound by a scan. You have not looked at it yet.

        
SavedYou want to keep it.

        
Ready to applyClaude has built your application and it is waiting for you to send it.

        
AppliedYou sent it. Only you set this, in the dropdown or by telling Claude 'I applied'. Claude never marks it Applied on its own.

        
InterviewingYou are in a hiring process.

        
OfferYou have an offer.

        
PassedYou are not interested.

        
ClosedThe posting is gone or the process ended.

      
    

  

  
Ready to apply and Applied are different: Ready to apply means the ball is with you. Applied means you sent it.

  
Some Daybook jobs show "Employer hidden (Daybook)" in the Org column. Daybook shows the employer only to paid members. The link still shows a short description and often the salary. To build an application for one, paste the description into Claude, and tell it the employer if you know it. Claude will not guess.


  
If something goes wrong

  

    
    
      
  • The scan stalled. A prompt is waiting for an answer. Check that the task's Permission mode is still Auto (Scheduled, your task, Edit). Then open the run from the task's History list and answer the prompt. If it keeps stopping, tell Nadeen.
    • 
      
  • The app shows a yellow "sign in again" banner. Sign in again, then click Run now once.
    • 
      
  • It did not run. The app was closed or the Mac was asleep. Open the app and wake the Mac. One catch-up run happens on its own. Also check that Keep computer awake is on.
    • 
      
  • The summary says "no LinkedIn alert emails". No alert emails arrived from LinkedIn in the last few days. Set up the LinkedIn job alerts from Level 4, with email delivery.
    • 
      
  • The run was rejected. You are at your Claude plan's usage limit. Wait for it to reset.
