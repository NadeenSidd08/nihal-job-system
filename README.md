# Job search with Claude

This helps you find journalism jobs, track them in a Google Sheet, and tailor your résumé, cover letter, and clips for each one with Claude.

## How to start

You start from the onboarding page Nadeen sends you (link to be added). Follow it step by step. You don't need to set anything up from this page.

## What you need

- The Claude desktop app on a Mac
- A paid Claude plan
- Gmail and Google Sheets
- Your résumé
- Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF
- A link to your website

## The three commands

| Command | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `/jobs` | A simple menu that points you to the right next step | Coming soon |
| `/find-jobs` | Finds journalism jobs that fit you and adds them to your Google Sheet | Coming soon |
| `/apply` | Tailors your résumé, writes a cover letter, and picks clips for one job | Coming soon |

## Privacy

Your files stay on your Mac and in your Google account. Personal files, like your profile, résumé, and clips, are never committed to this repo.
